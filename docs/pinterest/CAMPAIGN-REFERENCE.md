## Location truthfulness

Villa Fabulosa is the Temecula property. Use location variants as follows:

- Temecula Wine Country: the property may be described as being in Temecula Wine Country.
- Riverside County: describe it as being in Temecula, Riverside County.
- Southern California: the regional location is appropriate.
- Los Angeles: address travelers **from Los Angeles**. Do not imply the estate is located there or promise an unverified driving time.
- Orange County: address travelers **from Orange County**. Do not imply the estate is located there or promise an unverified driving time.

Keep copy natural when using a plural search phrase to describe this single estate. Do not imply a portfolio of properties or add amenities, proximity claims, rankings, savings, fee exemptions or guarantees merely to match a keyword/headline.
